# Mission

## Why this site exists

Olavo is looking for paid work: a job, a PJ contract or a consulting
engagement. The way in is proof, not a CV. He builds and runs production AI
systems with a team of specialised agents, and he does it fast.
(See `~/Work/operation-get-a-job/AGENTS.md`.)

The first target is **Jotha Prime**, a Senior Sistemas consultancy in
Serra/Vitória, ES. It just took investment (the BossaNova ecosystem) and is
turning itself from a service company into a product company (JothaX,
JothaZap). It sells AI and MCP, but its team is mostly Senior functional
consultants. It needs **engineering capacity for AI products that run in
production**.

The decision-maker is **Caúca**. He is a hands-on infrastructure and ERP
person, he follows Olavo on Instagram, and his own reel is titled "IA sem
engenharia é só apresentação". A DM is too small to carry a video, two
projects and a pilot idea. A link to one page carries all of it, and he can
forward it to Thalita (CEO).

## The page's one job

> Make Caúca reply "bora tomar esse café".

Everything on the page serves that. If a section doesn't help him decide to
spend 20 minutes with Olavo, it goes.

## Who reads it

1. **Caúca**, on his phone, in Instagram's in-app browser, probably between
   other things. He has 30–90 seconds of attention before deciding to watch
   the video. He knows infrastructure and ERP well, so concrete engineering
   details (cost caps, Spot VMs, monitoring, traceability) earn respect and
   buzzwords lose it.
2. **Thalita**, if he forwards it. She is the business and HCM side, so the
   pilot idea and the offer ladder matter more to her than the stack.

## What the page must say

1. **I build AI that runs in production, built and operated by agents.**
   That's engineering, not slides, which echoes his own line.
2. **Proof:** Plantão (messy official PDFs become 5,063 validated,
   traceable questions in under 30 hours) and Usher (a national event
   catalog with a public API, running in the cloud with monitoring and a
   cost cap).
3. **The bridge:** turning messy documents into validated data is exactly
   what Jotha's Senior clients still do by hand (NF-e, contracts, HR
   documents).
4. **One pilot idea for Jotha:** a document-intake agent. A client sends a
   photo or PDF on WhatsApp (where JothaX already lives), and the agent
   turns it into validated data loaded into Senior, with a review queue and
   traceability.
5. **A small first step:** a 20-minute coffee in Serra. Behind it, a ladder:
   a fixed-price diagnostic, then a pilot, then an embedded engineer or a
   white-label partnership.

## What success looks like

- **The page:** Caúca opens it, watches the video and replies.
- **The campaign:** a call or coffee with Caúca (and ideally Thalita), then
  a paid diagnostic or an offer.

## Beyond Jotha

The site is olavostauros.com, so it will outlive this pitch. Build the pitch
page so the next one (another company, another person) is a copy with new
copy and a new pilot idea, not a rewrite. Don't build the rest of the site
until the pitch is out.
